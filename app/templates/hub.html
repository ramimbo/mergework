Future path

MRWK starts as a native project coin on the MergeWork ledger. Supported paths today are GitHub balance claims, linked mrwk1 wallet payouts, and signed wallet-to-wallet transfers.

MergeWork does not currently operate a public BTC, USDC, fiat, bridge, exchange, or off-ramp. Future public snapshots, bridges, and onchain claims require separate maintainer/contributor discussion before implementation.

Accounts and payouts

Accepted work can be paid to a linked native wallet address. Wallets use public keys and signed transfer transactions to move MRWK inside the ledger.
